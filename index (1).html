05 / FAQ

Questions authors usually ask.

Do you work with self-published authors?

Yes. Services can be tailored to self-published, independently published and other author situations, depending on the project.

Can I purchase only one service?

Yes. Focused services are available when you have one specific marketing need. Larger campaigns can combine multiple services.

How do I know which service I need?

Start with a conversation about your book, goals and current marketing position. The proposal can then prioritize the most relevant work.

Are the investment ranges fixed?

No. They are indicative ranges. A final proposal depends on scope, deliverables, duration and the needs of the book.

Do you guarantee sales, rankings or reviews?

No. Marketing can improve discoverability and create opportunities, but sales, rankings and reader responses depend on many factors outside a marketer's control.
