About Deon

Marketing with intention.

A good book can exist for years without reaching the readers who would appreciate it most. The challenge is not always the book itself. Sometimes it is how the book is positioned, presented, discovered, and promoted.

My work focuses on identifying those opportunities and building practical marketing strategies around them.

I work with authors who want a clearer path from “my book is published” to “my book is being discovered.”
