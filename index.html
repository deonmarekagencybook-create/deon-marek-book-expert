ABOUT DEON

A practical marketing partner for authors.

I work with authors who want a clearer path from “my book is published” to “my book is being discovered.”

Great marketing is not about making every author do everything. It is about identifying the opportunities around a book and choosing the right combination of actions to strengthen its visibility and presentation.

My approach combines book positioning, retail-page thinking, discoverability, promotional assets, and reader-facing strategy. The goal is to build a marketing plan around the book itself rather than force every title into the same formula.

THE OBJECTIVE

Make the right readers more likely to find, notice, and consider your book.
