Book Marketing Guide

How to think about your book marketing investment.

If you have started looking into book marketing, you may have noticed something quickly: pricing can vary dramatically.

One service may focus on Amazon visibility. Another may concentrate on reader outreach, promotional assets, book clubs, publicity, or campaign management.

That means comparing prices alone does not always tell you what you are actually receiving.

“The better question is what am I actually getting for my investment, and how does it support my book's discovery?”

What should you look at before investing?

Before committing to a larger marketing campaign, consider whether the foundations of the book's presentation are ready.

  • Is the cover competitive within the genre?
  • Does the title and subtitle clearly communicate what the book is about?
  • Is the book description written to attract the intended reader?
  • Are the Amazon categories and keywords relevant?
  • Does the author biography support the book's positioning?
  • Is the pricing appropriate for the book and its market?
  • Is there a clear understanding of the intended readership?
  • Is the author's website or book landing page ready?
  • Is there a way to stay connected with readers after they discover the book?

Why strategy matters.

A book does not necessarily need every available marketing service. It needs the right combination of activities for its current situation.

For one author, the priority may be improving retail positioning. For another, it may be reader discovery, outreach, promotional assets, or a broader campaign.

The starting point should always be the book and the audience it is trying to reach.
