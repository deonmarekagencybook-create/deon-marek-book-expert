About

Marketing starts with understanding the book.

A published book can have a strong story, valuable ideas, or a compelling concept and still struggle to reach the readers who would appreciate it most.

My work focuses on the areas between publication and discovery: how a book is positioned, how readers encounter it, how its retail presence communicates its value, and where additional promotional opportunities may exist.

Rather than treating every title exactly the same, I look at the individual book and build marketing support around its genre, audience, goals, and current opportunities.

Strategy. Visibility. Discovery. A practical approach to helping authors create stronger opportunities for their books.
